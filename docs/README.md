# AURELION docs

AURELION is an AI coding agent that lives in your terminal. These pages explain everything it does, in reading order: start at the top, or jump to what you need.

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): install, activate, connect a model, and run your first turn.
2. [The cockpit](cockpit.md): the stream, the rail, the layouts, the flow view and the themes.
3. [Keys](keys.md): every key, and when it acts.
4. [Commands](commands.md): every slash command, and how to write your own.
5. [Modes and safety](modes-and-safety.md): the four modes, approvals, trusted folders, rules, the sandbox and spend caps.
6. [Undo and review](undo-and-review.md): snapshots, undo, the review pane and the checks after each turn.
7. [Models and providers](models.md): 84 providers, Claude and ChatGPT plans, fallbacks and profiles.
8. [Tools](tools.md): the built-in tools, extension tools, MCP servers, language servers and plugins.
9. [Agents](agents.md): subagents, parallel workers, your own agents, review, debate and swarms.
10. [Memory and context](memory-and-context.md): instructions, memory, key facts, compaction, past sessions and skills.
11. [Automation](automation.md): headless runs, CI, batch runs, scheduled jobs, kanban, hooks and goals.
12. [Serve and chat](serve-and-chat.md): the daemon, chat platforms, webhooks, phone calls, the API and Agent2Agent.
13. [Editors and devices](editors-and-devices.md): ACP for editors, AURELION as an MCP server, device nodes, voice and containers.
14. [Configuration](configuration.md): the config files and their layers, and every section of settings.
15. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): `aurelion doctor`, logs, crash reports and common problems.
16. [Security and releases](security-and-releases.md): signed releases, updates, your license and keys, and what AURELION sends where.
